Welcome To Our Hotel

Deluxe Hotels & Resorts is a quiet place to stay well: comfortable rooms with garden and pool views, an outdoor pool, a restaurant that serves through the evening, and a team that is here around the clock. Whether you are here for a weekend away, a family trip or a longer break, the hotel is built around slow mornings and easy days.

Book direct on this site for the best rate — no account needed, free cancellation until two days before check-in, and the choice to pay at the hotel or online. Families love the Family Suite, which sleeps four with a garden-facing balcony and the kids' area just steps away.

24/7 Front Desk

Our front desk never closes — check in late, ask for a late check-out, or get help with anything during your stay.

Restaurant Bar

Dinner and drinks in our restaurant, served through the evening. Guests can charge meals to the room and settle everything at check-out.

Transfer Services

Arriving late or leaving early? Ask the front desk about transfers and we will arrange a ride for you.

Spa Suites

Unwind after a day out — ask about our spa services and suites when you book or at check-in.
