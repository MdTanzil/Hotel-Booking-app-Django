Everything included with your stay

Every booking at Deluxe Hotels & Resorts includes the following — no surprises at check-out.

In your room

  • Free high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the hotel
  • Daily housekeeping
  • Complimentary bottled water
  • Air conditioning, flat-screen TV and mini fridge
  • Coffee maker, in-room safe and hair dryer

Around the hotel

  • Outdoor swimming pool and kids' area
  • Garden access — many rooms have a garden-facing balcony
  • Restaurant serving breakfast, lunch and dinner
  • 24/7 front desk and room service

When you book direct

  • Free cancellation until 2 days before check-in
  • Pay at the hotel or online — your choice
  • No account needed to book
  • Members save 10% on rooms when signed in, plus late check-out until 1 PM and a free room upgrade when available
  • Your booking reference and confirmation by email

Good to know

  • Check-in from 2:00 PM, check-out by 12:00 PM
  • Extra services such as spa treatments and transfers can be arranged at the front desk
  • See the Policies & FAQ page for cancellation, payment and house rules
